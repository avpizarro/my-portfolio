# About Alejandra Valdes Pizarro

## Professional Summary
Full Stack Developer with commercial experience building and supporting production web applications using Ruby on Rails, Vue, and Vite. Alejandra also has experience investigating production issues, working with CI/CD pipelines, mentoring interns, and developing an AI-powered knowledge-assistant proof of concept using RAG, LLM APIs, and Kubernetes.

## Career Background
Before moving into software development, Alejandra worked in technical product development for performance apparel. At 2XU, she created technical product specifications and adopted digital 3D sampling to reduce development time and material waste. At MAAP, she helped implement a Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system across product development. These experiences showed her how thoughtfully introduced technology can improve everyday work and gave her practical experience supporting digital transformation.

After completing a Web Development Bootcamp at Monash University, Alejandra moved into a Software Developer role at Pronto Software in 2025. She now delivers features for an enterprise Ruby on Rails, Vue, and Vite application; investigates live customer issues involving ERP integrations and data synchronisation; supports deployment reliability; reviews code and solution designs; and mentors interns.

## What She's Building Now
Alongside her production software work, Alejandra is exploring AI-powered applications. She developed a knowledge-assistant proof of concept using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), LLM APIs, and Kubernetes, and this portfolio chatbot is another practical example of that interest.
